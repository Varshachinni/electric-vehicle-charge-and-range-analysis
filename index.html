Strategic Overview Insights

Our comprehensive analysis of 50,000 charging sessions across major charging networks reveals an average range delivery of approximately 350 km. Interestingly, weather conditions (cold/rainy vs clear) show minor variations in average energy consumed, proving battery thermal management units function efficiently to offset range drops. The distribution of charging ports is evenly split, with CCS2 and CHAdeMO connectors representing the highest volume, validating high infrastructure compatibility across various electric passenger vehicles.

Scenario 1: Aarav – City Energy Planner

Aarav is responsible for managing electricity demand across urban areas. He analyzes charging patterns (time of day, duration, power consumed) to locate peak hours, identify high-demand neighborhoods via heatmaps, and plan charger infrastructure extensions for grid stability.

Aarav's Grid Planning Recommendations

  • Target High-Demand Zones: Pune East leads in average power consumed (52.58 kWh), followed by Mumbai Central (49.61 kWh). Future charger installations should be expedited in these regions.
  • Charger Placement: Level 1 (Slow) and Level 2 (Standard) standard chargers capture the largest market share. Installing fast DC chargers in commercial areas could relieve city peak load bottlenecks.
  • Time Peak Optimization: Morning, Afternoon, and Evening slots hold similar portions of demand. Peak evening energy draws represent a key opportunity to implement dynamic grid pricing (Time of Use rates) to smooth utility loading.

Fleet Battery Health Assessment Report

Charge session durations are uniformly spread across intervals (10 to 140 minutes), pointing to consistent vehicle cycling and scheduled charging shifts. Sector workloads are highest in Mumbai and Pune hubs. To maintain peak health, fleet vehicles in high-temperature areas require pre-conditioned active cooling during DC quick charges. Vehicles in Pune East are operating with slightly lower charging efficiencies (92.9%), indicating a need for preemptive cooling circuit checks.

Ravi's Consumer Decisions

Vehicle stats show Tesla Model 3 (12,671 sessions) leads, followed closely by Nissan Leaf, Tata Nexon EV, and Hyundai Ioniq 5. Driving styles are evenly distributed among Sport, Normal, and Eco modes. Regional range delivered is extremely stable across networks at ~350 km. High battery capacity and adaptive regenerative braking systems in models like the Hyundai Ioniq 5 offer consumers the best cost-to-performance ratio for regional highway commutes.
